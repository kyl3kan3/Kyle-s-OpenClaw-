## Posting Tips for Facebook

1. **Use images or video.** Posts with visual content get 2-3x more engagement than text-only.
2. **Ask questions.** Facebook's algorithm boosts posts that generate comments.
3. **Tag and share.** Encourage "tag someone who needs this" for organic reach.
4. **Post 1-2 times per day.** Consistency over volume.
5. **Best posting times.** Wednesday 11 AM and 1 PM, Thursday-Friday 1-4 PM.
6. **Join and contribute to groups.** Provide value before promoting. Follow group rules strictly.
7. **Use Facebook Stories.** Stories appear at the top of the feed and have high visibility.
8. **Boost top-performing organic posts.** Don't run ads on new content — boost what's already working.
