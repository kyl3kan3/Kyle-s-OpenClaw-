## Posting Tips for TikTok

1. **Post consistently.** 1-2 times per day for growth. Minimum 3-4 times per week.
2. **Use trending sounds.** Check the Discover page daily for audio trends.
3. **Hook in the first 2 seconds.** Open with a bold statement or question.
4. **Add captions/text overlays.** 80% of users watch without sound.
5. **Engage with comments.** Reply with video responses for extra content.
6. **Use 3-5 relevant hashtags.** Mix popular (#fyp, #adulting) with niche (#projectadulting).
7. **Post at peak times.** Best times: 7-9 AM, 12-3 PM, 7-11 PM (user's local time zone).
8. **Leverage duets and stitches.** Engage with related creators for cross-audience exposure.
